# Store Policies

## Shipping

We ship domestic orders within 2 to 5 business days. Delivery times may vary during peak sale periods or severe weather. We do not promise same-day shipping.

## Returns

Unworn items with original tags can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Final-sale items and socks are not eligible for return.

## Exchanges

Size exchanges are allowed within 30 days of delivery if the requested variant is still in stock. Color exchanges are treated as a new order after the original return is approved.

## Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. Cash on delivery and cryptocurrency are not supported.
