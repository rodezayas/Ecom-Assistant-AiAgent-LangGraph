# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you have a physical store?

Alta Norma Fashion currently sells online only.

## Do you offer international shipping?

Not at this time. Orders are limited to domestic shipping zones supported by the store.

## Can I change my size after ordering?

You can request a size exchange if the new size is available and the item is eligible under the exchange policy.

## Do you offer custom discounts?

Only published promotions apply. The assistant cannot invent discounts or special pricing.
